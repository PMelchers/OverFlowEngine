# Brag Plan: OverFlowEngine — Vacation Planner flow (restored)

**What:** OverFlowEngine's built-in Vacation Planner template: one form in, a full trip out.
**Angle:** The camera rides the data. A green pulse travels the real Vacation Planner flow from `templates.ts`, and the camera punches in on each block as it fires.
**Tone:** default; camera-follows-the-data product tour. 20s, 1920x1080. Music: happy-beats vol-1.
**Grounded copy:** block names and labels come from `templates.ts` and `CostSummaryCard.tsx`. The itinerary lines and euro amounts are fictional demo content. The template's seed variables and AI Input blocks are left out to keep it to 10 blocks.

## Storyboard
1. **Form (0–3s):** Plan My Trip fills in (Italy, car, hiking/museums/local food, hotel, 2000 EUR). "Plan a vacation. Draw the flow." Submit at 3.0s.
2. **Destination (3–7s):** Pick Destination Agent → destinationAddress "Florence, Italy". "An agent picks the place."
3. **Itinerary (6–9.5s):** Vacation Planner Agent → vacationPlan lines. "Another writes the itinerary."
4. **Routes (9.5–12s):** Suggest Activities, then Google + Apple Maps routes framed together. "Real Google + Apple Maps routes."
5. **Cost + PDF (12–17.5s):** Trip Cost rows, then the Trip PDF. "Cost? Handled." / "Out comes a Trip PDF."
6. **Pull-back (17.5–20s):** the whole chain, every block green; the logo and tagline.
